## Two Tracks for Initial Engagement

---

## Track 1: ER/OR Nurse Cousins (Discovery)

Casual conversation - you're learning, not selling.

### Pain Points

1. "What documentation do you hate doing the most?"
2. "When it gets busy, what paperwork gets skipped or rushed?"
3. "What happens when documentation is incomplete? What problems does it cause?"
4. "What do you wish someone would automate for you?"

### Process

5. "Walk me through what you document for a typical patient - from arrival to handoff"
6. "Where do errors usually happen?"
7. "What gets you in trouble with compliance?"

### Validation

8. "If AI could fill out [X form] and you just had to verify it's correct - would that help?"
9. "What would need to be true for you to trust it?"

---

## Track 2: Nurse Consultant (Structure + Partnership)

More formal - exploring business opportunity.

### Workflow Structure

1. "What are the standard documentation workflows in [ER/OR/clinic]?"
2. "What are the compliance requirements that can't be skipped?"
3. "What does 'gold standard' documentation look like?"

### Market Opportunity

4. "Where do hospitals/clinics struggle most with documentation compliance?"
5. "What costs them money when documentation fails? (Audits, liability, denied claims)"
6. "Is this a problem they'd pay to solve?"

### Partnership Pitch

7. "I'm building an AI execution management platform - it enforces workflows so nothing gets skipped."
8. "I need domain expertise to define the 'correct' workflows. Would you be interested in a revenue share partnership?"
9. "You'd define the workflows, I'd build the enforcement. You'd get a cut of every sale."

```
WEEK 1 (Parallel):
├── Cousins: Casual calls - discover pain points

WEEK 2:
├── Synthesize: What problems came up repeatedly?

WEEK 3+:
└── If aligned: Draft partnership agreement
```

## What Healthcare AI Execution Management Actually Is

**Use this to explain the concept:**

**NOT:** AI doing clinical work (diagnosis, treatment, medical judgment)

**IS:** AI doing documentation/compliance tasks with enforcement so nothing gets missed

| AI Does This... | Isagawa Enforces... |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Fills documentation forms | All required fields present |
| Generates protocol checklists | All items checked before proceed |
| Drafts handoff notes | Required info included |
| Fills compliance paperwork | Validated before submission |

**Key insight:** The value isn't AI filling forms. The value is ENFORCEMENT - ensuring nothing gets skipped, even when busy.
