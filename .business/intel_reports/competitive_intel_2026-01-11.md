**NIS2 (Network and Information Security Directive 2):**
- Personal liability for directors
- Critical infrastructure must have security + governance
- In force NOW

**DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act):**
- In force since January 17, 2025
- Financial services must prove operational resilience
- AI governance = part of resilience

**Isagawa angle:** "NIS2 + DORA already require governance. EU AI Act coming 2027. Start now."
