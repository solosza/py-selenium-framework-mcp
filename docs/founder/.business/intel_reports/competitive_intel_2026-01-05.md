## Closest Rival: Credo AI

**Threat Score: 6/10**

Why closest:
- Full AI governance platform with policy workflows
- Supports EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 compliance
- Produces audit-ready artifacts (model cards, impact assessments)
- Ranked #1 in Forrester for policy management

| Feature | Credo AI | Isagawa |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Step-by-step workflow | No | Yes |
| Non-bypassable gates | No (recommendations) | Yes (mandatory) |
| Human escalation triggers | Limited (alerts) | Core feature |
| Non-tech verticals | Limited | Yes |
| Standalone product | Yes | Yes |
| Execution enforcement | No (observation) | Yes |

**Gap:** Credo AI focuses on **policy and compliance documentation** - it helps you document and track AI governance. Isagawa enforces **how AI actually executes work** at runtime. Credo watches; Isagawa controls.

---

## Second Closest: Holistic AI

**Threat Score: 5/10**

Why close:
- End-to-end AI governance platform
- Shadow AI detection
- LLM auditing (bias, hallucinations, toxicity)
- EU AI Act risk classification dashboard

**Gap:** Holistic AI is a **monitoring and audit platform**. It detects problems after they happen. Isagawa prevents problems by enforcing execution patterns before and during AI work.

---

## Gap: What NO Competitor Offers

- **Step-by-step execution enforcement** - All competitors observe; none enforce workflow steps
- **Non-bypassable gates (mandatory)** - Competitors offer recommendations; Isagawa makes gates mandatory
- **Human escalation triggers (built-in)** - Competitors alert; Isagawa pauses and escalates
- **Non-tech vertical specialization** - Competitors target tech teams; Isagawa targets healthcare, finance, construction
- **Management layer (not security)** - Competitors focus on risk/compliance; Isagawa focuses on execution control
- **Vendor agnostic** - Works across any AI provider, not locked to one ecosystem
