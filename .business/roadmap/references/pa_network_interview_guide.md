# Prior Authorization Network Interview Guide

**Purpose:** Validate 20% knowledge gaps + identify Pack Contributor candidates
**Target:** Medical Assistants / Hospital Admin staff in your network
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes
**Tone:** Casual, informational, NOT sales-y

---

## Pre-Call Preparation

**Context Setting:**
- This is a warm intro call with someone in your network
- Goal: Learn real-world PA workflow, NOT sell anything
- Secondary goal: Get introduced to their PA Manager (Pack Contributor candidate)

**What to Have Ready:**
- Notepad/doc for capturing findings
- Post-call documentation template (see end of guide)

---

## Interview Script

### Opening (2 min)

**Script:**
"Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time. I'm exploring a project around AI governance for prior authorization, and I wanted to get your perspective on how PA actually works day-to-day. I'm not selling anything - just trying to understand the real workflow and pain points. Is now still a good time?"

**If they ask "What's AI governance?":**
"It's basically making sure AI tools are transparent, compliant, and fair - especially important in healthcare where there's HIPAA, bias concerns, and patient safety. But I'm here to learn, not pitch anything."

---

### Section 1: Current Workflow (5-7 min)

**Goal:** Understand their daily PA process vs documented process.

### Section 2: Pain Points & Failures (5-7 min)

**Goal:** Identify where current process breaks down.

#### 1. Denials

"What percentage of your PAs get denied on first submission? Just a rough guess is fine."
- [ ] Denial rate estimate: ___%

"What are the most common reasons for denial?"
- [ ] Top denial reasons:
  1. _______________
  2. _______________
  3. _______________

"How do you know WHY a PA was denied? Is it clear from the payer?"
- [ ] Denial transparency: Clear / Vague / Unclear

#### 2. Missing Information

"How often do you submit a PA and then realize you're missing something?"
- [ ] Frequency: Rarely / Sometimes / Often

"What documentation is hardest to find or gather?"
- [ ] Hardest docs: _______________

#### 3. Payer Differences

"Which insurance companies are easiest to work with for PA?"
- [ ] Easiest payers: _______________

"Which are the worst? Why?"
- [ ] Worst payers: _______________
- [ ] Why: _______________

"Do different payers have totally different processes, or is it similar across the board?"
- [ ] Process variation: Similar / Very different / Somewhere in between

#### 4. Delays

"What causes the longest delays in getting a PA approved?"
- [ ] Delay causes: _______________

"How long does it typically take to get a PA back? Hours, days, weeks?"
- [ ] Typical turnaround: _______________

"Do urgent or expedited PAs actually get processed faster?"
- [ ] Expedited PAs: Yes, faster / No, same / Sometimes

### Section 3: AI/Automation Experience (3-5 min)

**Goal:** Understand current AI adoption and trust.

#### 1. Current AI Tools

"Has your hospital started using any AI or automation for PA?"
- [ ] AI adoption: Yes / No / Piloting

**If Yes:**
"How's it working? Does it actually save time?"
- [ ] Effectiveness: _______________

**If No:**
"Why not? What's holding you back?"
- [ ] Barriers: _______________

#### 2. Trust & Concerns

"If an AI tool suggested approving or denying a PA, would you trust it?"
- [ ] Trust level: Yes / No / Depends

"What would make you NOT trust it?"
- [ ] Trust blockers: _______________

"What would you want to see or know before trusting AI for PA?"
- [ ] Requirements for trust: _______________

#### 3. Ideal Future State

"If you could wave a magic wand, what would the ideal PA process look like?"
- [ ] Ideal state: _______________

"What parts should be automated vs require human review?"
- [ ] Automate: _______________
- [ ] Human review needed: _______________

### Section 4: Decision-Makers & Introductions (3 min)

**Goal:** Get introduced to PA Manager/Director (Pack Contributor candidate).

#### 1. Organizational Structure

"Who manages the PA team at your hospital?"
- [ ] Manager name/title: _______________

"Is there a PA coordinator or director role?"
- [ ] Title: _______________

"Who decides which tools or systems you use for PA?"
- [ ] Decision-maker: _______________

#### 2. Introduction Request

**Script:**
"Would your manager be open to a short call with me? I'm exploring how AI governance could help with PA compliance and transparency."

- [ ] Response: Yes / No / Maybe

**If Yes or Maybe:**
"I'm specifically looking for PA experts who might want to help shape how AI should work in this space - any chance your manager would be interested in that kind of partnership?"

- [ ] Interest level: _______________

**Next step:**
"Could you send them a quick intro email? Or give me their email and I'll reach out mentioning you?"

- [ ] Contact info / intro method: _______________
