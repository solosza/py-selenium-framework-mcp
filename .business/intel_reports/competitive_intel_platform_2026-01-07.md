## Gap: What NO Competitor Offers

- **Step-by-step execution enforcement** (not just input/output validation)
- **Non-bypassable gates** (mandatory checkpoints, not optional)
- **Human escalation triggers** (built into workflow, not afterthought)
- **Domain-specific Execution Engines** (vertical expertise encoded)
- **Management layer** (not security/safety layer)
- **Vendor agnostic** (works with any AI, not tied to specific LLM)
- **Protocols + Smart Gates architecture** (guidance + enforcement separated)
